The problem we solve

When a recruit finishes, the hours live in a binder, attendance is on paper, and proving who completed what means digging through a filing cabinet. B4C Academy keeps the record — complete, current, and ready to hand to the state.

Every competitor digitizes the work. B4C does some of the work — and remembers everything so you don't have to. AI always drafts. A human always approves. That's the safety system and the trust model.
